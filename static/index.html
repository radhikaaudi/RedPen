Reading the handwriting

OCR that understands the page

Redpen's OCR is NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, the vision model Nebius Token Factory serves for document understanding. It doesn't just turn pixels into text — it reads an answer sheet the way a teacher does.

Plain OCR gives you

  • ✗Lines of text in reading order, with no idea which answer belongs to which question
  • ✗Crossed-out words read as if they were part of the answer
  • ✗Drawings and working ignored
  • ✗Nothing that tells a teacher “check this one”

Super VL gives Redpen

Super VL
  • ✓Every page in one call, each answer matched to the question number the student wrote
  • ✓Word-for-word, spelling mistakes kept — the grade depends on them
  • ✓Crossed-out words left out; unreadable words become [?]
  • ✓A legibility rating per answer, so hard-to-read ones are flagged
  • ✓Diagrams and working described

Marking like a teacher

Marks the working, not just the answer

Nemotron 3 Ultra checks a student's working line by line and judges whether their thinking was heading the right way. Python then decides which answers go back to you. From the maths sample class:

Step marking

Marks kept
  1. 1Profit = 15/100 x 2400✓
  2. 2= 340 (should be 360)✗
  3. 3SP = CP + profit = 2400 + 340✓
  4. 4= Rs 2740✗

Arjun's method was right, but he slipped on 15% of 2400. He keeps the method marks, including step 3, which correctly uses his wrong value (error carried forward): 2/4, not 0. A different valid method, such as 115% of 2400, earns full marks.

Sentiment

Right directionAnswer ✗
🧭 Teacher intervention. Right thinking, wrong answer.

Sentiment is the direction of a student's thinking: right, partly right or wrong, judged apart from the final answer. When the thinking was right but the answer came out wrong, Redpen asks you to step in, because a quick word fixes a slip faster than a red cross.
