Redpen grades handwritten answer sheets

For teachers · Built on NVIDIA Nemotron

Photograph the sheets. Redpen reads the handwriting, marks every rubric point with the student's own words as evidence, flags anything it is unsure about, and tells you what the class misunderstood. You stay in charge of every mark.
